01 — About me

About Me

I'm Arun Yadav, a 32-year-old Full-Stack developer based in New Delhi, India. With over 10 years of experience in the software industry, I've had the opportunity to work on a different domain including FinTech, MedTech, EdTech, Embedded Systems, and Aerospace & Defence. This gave me opportunity to develop expertise in a range of technologies and frameworks, including Javascript, JAVA, Swift3, PHP, MySQL, MongoDB, AngularJS, Angular, NodeJS, Android, Ionic Framework, Jersey2.0, TweenMax, ExpressJS, SocketIO, WordPress, Magento, and Codeignitor.

What I love most about my work is the opportunity to constantly learn and explore new domain and technologies. I'm always on the lookout for interesting challenges and opportunities to expand my skill set.

In my previous roles, I've had the privilege of working with some truly brilliant minds, including at Newgen Payments, Lattice Innovations, Electronut Labs, Simplilearn, Zenius. Currently, I am working at Threye Interactive, where I am leading a talented group of developers and help them achieve their full potential.

When I'm not coding, you can usually find me tinkering with hardware projects, or exploring the latest tech trends. I'm passionate about using technology to solve real-world problems, and I'm always excited to connect with others who share that vision.
